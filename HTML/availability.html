Three Ways To Run It options

A booking engine

Lodgify, OwnerRez, Hostaway or Uplisting. Syncs automatically with Airbnb and VRBO so the calendar can never drift, and takes payment properly. Costs money — roughly $20–40 a month for one property — and does the most by a distance.

A free iCal feed

Airbnb or VRBO, embedded read-only. Publish your listing calendar as an iCal link, subscribe to it in Google Calendar, and embed that. Free, updates itself, and plainer to look at — but perfectly honest, which is the part that matters.

No calendar at all

Just ask us. For a single cabin this is a completely reasonable choice, and better than a calendar you forget to update. I would replace the panel above with a short “send us your dates” block and make the booking form the whole page.
