Availability

When Is It Free? calendar

A live calendar goes here

This is the one thing on the site that has to be right, because a calendar that is out of date is worse than no calendar at all — it produces enquiries for dates you have already sold.

The usual answer is a booking engine that syncs with Airbnb and VRBO automatically: Lodgify, OwnerRez, Hostaway or Uplisting. They give you an embed code for a calendar and a real “Book” button that takes payment. A free alternative is to publish your Airbnb calendar as an iCal feed and embed it read-only through Google Calendar — less pretty, but honest and automatic.
