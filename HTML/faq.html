Frequently Asked faq

The questions guests actually email about. If you find yourself answering something twice, tell me and it goes on this page.

Is it cheaper to book here than on Airbnb?

Yes. Listing sites add a guest service fee of roughly 12–15% on top of the nightly rate. Booking here does not, so the same stay costs less. TELL CLAUDE: confirm this is how you want it phrased.

Do I need a car?

TELL CLAUDE: almost certainly yes, but say whether guests can walk into the village and whether AWD is needed in winter.

Can we bring our dog?

TELL CLAUDE: policy, fee, size limits, whether the yard is fenced, and whether dogs can be left alone in the cabin. Dog owners plan whole trips around this answer.

What is the Wi-Fi like? Can I work from here?

TELL CLAUDE: actual speed if you know it, and whether video calls hold up. Be honest here - a guest who plans a work week around bad Wi-Fi leaves a bad review.

Is there mobile signal?

TELL CLAUDE: which carriers work at the cabin. Mountain valleys are patchy and this catches people out.

Do we need a park reservation?

Rocky Mountain National Park has run a timed-entry system during peak season in recent years, booked ahead on recreation.gov. The dates change year to year, so check before you travel. TELL CLAUDE: add this season’s specifics if you know them.

What is the minimum stay?

TELL CLAUDE: minimums by season, and whether you ever flex on them midweek or last minute. See the rates page for the full picture.

What if I need to cancel?

TELL CLAUDE: your cancellation terms in one plain sentence. Full details are on the rates page.

Is the altitude going to be a problem?

Grand Lake sits at roughly 8,400 feet. Guests arriving from sea level often feel it for the first day — headaches, poor sleep, getting winded easily. Drink more water than you think you need, take the first evening gently, and leave the big hike for day two.
