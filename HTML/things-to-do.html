The area

Grand Lake & The Park explore

Grand Lake is the quiet western gateway to Rocky Mountain National Park — a boardwalk village on Colorado’s largest natural lake, with the Never Summer range behind it. This page is the local knowledge a listing site cannot give your guests.

Season by season

All Year Round seasons

Summer

Trail Ridge Road is open, the lake is busy, and the park runs its timed-entry reservation system. Book park entry ahead on recreation.gov. TELL CLAUDE: anything you tell summer guests that is not obvious.

Fall

Elk rut and aspen colour, roughly late September. Quieter than summer and, for a lot of people, the best week of the year to come. TELL CLAUDE: your best guess at peak colour week, and where you send people to see the elk.

Winter

Trail Ridge Road closes, so Grand Lake becomes a destination rather than a through-route. Snowmobiling, snowshoeing and cross-country skiing; Winter Park is the nearest downhill. TELL CLAUDE: snowmobile rental you recommend, whether guests can store gear at the cabin, and how the driveway handles snow.

Spring

The quiet shoulder. Snow lingers on the higher trails well into June and some businesses close between seasons — but rates are lower and you will have the place to yourself. TELL CLAUDE: honestly, is spring a good time to come? If it is not, say so - guests trust a host who tells them.
